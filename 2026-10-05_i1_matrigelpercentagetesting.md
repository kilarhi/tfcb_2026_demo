## RR2- Matrigel % Testing on nTSCs
#### 10-5-2026 issue <https://github.com/kilarhi/tfcb_2026_demo/issues/1>
## Reagents 
| Reagent    | Vendor | Catalog # | Stock Concentration | Final Concentration |
| --------   | -------| --------   | -------| -------- |
| Y-27632  | Selleck | S1049 | 10 mM | 10 µM | 
| Emricasan | Selleck  | S7775 | 50 mM | 5 µM |
| Advanced DMEM/F-12  | Thermoversion | 12634010|
## Procedure 
1. Prep: Warm TSC media to 37C. Get ice bucket and thaw on ice the gels you will need from the -80C. (You will need 25µL of matrigel/droplet).Chill some P200 filter tips in the -30C freezer. Pre-warm the receiving plates (48-well plates) in the 37C incubator.
2. Dissociate cells with TrpLE, quench in Wash media.
3. Centrifuge at 300g for 3min, then resuspend in 500µL cold DMEM:F12 media.
4. Count cells with Trypan blue on Countess.
5. Aliquot 2K cells/ droplet into Low bind 1.5mL Eppendorf tubes (1 for each gel condition).
6. Centrifuge at 300g for 3min.
7. Carefully pipette off as much of the supernatant as you can with a P200 pipette, leaving only ~10µL of media on the cell pellet.
8. Put the cell pellet on ice for ~5 min to chill before adding gel!!
9. Once ready, using pre-chilled pipette tips add gel to the cell pellet and pipette up and down ~5-8 times to resuspend evenly while avoiding bubbles. Keep on ice as often as possible to prevent gelling!! Keep ice bucket in hood and everything on ice during plating.
10. Plate 25µL droplets into each well.
11. As soon as plating is done, put plate into the 37C incubator to harden the gel into domes.
12. Incubate 15-30min at 37C to solidify gel.
13. Gently add 250µL TSC media on top of droplets by pipetting onto side of plate.
14. Return to incubator and grow in hypoxia.
